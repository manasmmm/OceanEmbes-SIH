Carbon credits for a CBG plant

From the hackathon submission: a 5 TPD compressed biogas plant running 330 days. Every input is an assumption to be replaced with measured data and the approved CCTS methodology. One Carbon Credit Certificate equals one tonne of CO₂e.

ER = BECH₄ + BEfossil − PE − LE  ·  PE = Egrid×EFgrid + Vdiesel×EFdiesel + (Mslip+Mleak+Mflare)×GWPCH₄ + PEdigestate

Methane-avoidance credits (BECH₄) come on top and depend on feedstock type and its baseline fate, so they are excluded here. Credits round down to whole tonnes (rule C7).
